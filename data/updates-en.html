Since the end of last year, we have updated the underlying system to improve facility browsing, map displays, and photos in Expo Maniac Map.

October 2026

Find facilities more easily

  • Use “Show all” to search across categories, including pavilions, shops, and rest areas.
  • Switch the facility list between “Nearby”, “Favorites”, and “Recently viewed” to find saved facilities or return to ones you have opened.
  • Mark facilities as visited or favorites directly from the list.

Use the map and lists more comfortably

  • Improved minimizing, maximizing, and dragging to resize the list and detail panels. Adjust facility information to a comfortable size while keeping the map in view.
  • Added 3D displays for vending machines, drinking water, benches, and playground equipment. Find the Forest of Tranquility in the rest category.
  • Refined map colors, world map and flag displays, and facility detail layouts on phones. Reopen these updates anytime with the “What’s new” button.

More photos and English information

  • Added facility photo thumbnails below the map. Scroll horizontally to browse facilities by their photos. Added photos of rest areas, toilets, and stages as well as pavilions.
  • Expanded English facility name data. Fixed menus, categories, and facilities with English names incorrectly appearing in Japanese when the browser was set to English.

Use venue data in other projects

  • October 19: Added 1,382 features, including roads, footpaths, steps, and traffic signals on Yumeshima, to the public dataset. All 4,832 features are available in GeoJSON, CSV, and OSM JSON.
  • Venue and facility data is now available as GeoJSON, CSV, and JSON. Search facilities on the data page and locate them on the map. You can also try a map of toilets, drinking water, and vending machines.
