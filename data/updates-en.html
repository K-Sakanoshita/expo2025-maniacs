We continue to improve Expo Maniac Map so you can keep exploring the venue.

October 2026

Open data

  • Venue and facility data is now available as GeoJSON, CSV, and JSON for map development, spatial analysis, and archival use.
  • Added 208 water and forest features and photo information. Try facility search and an interactive map of toilets, drinking water, and vending machines on the data page.

View Expo 2025 open data

Facility lists, search, and photos

  • Added “Show all” to the facility list. Open facilities on the Expo map directly from the public data search results.
  • Added photos for pavilions, Toilet 5, Rest Area 1, Water Plaza, EXPO Arena “Matsuri”, and a Pop-up Stage. Improved photo loading and scrolling guidance, and increased the display limit to 40 photos.
  • Menus and categories now follow your browser language. Added English facility names and display them when available in English mode.

Map display

  • Added 3D displays for vending machines, drinking water, benches, and playground equipment. Find the Forest of Tranquility in the rest category.
  • Adjusted map colors, world map and flag displays, and facility list layouts. Improved facility details on phones and map display while resizing panels.
  • Added the geometry of EXPO Arena “Matsuri” and included Water Plaza and Sea of Connections in the facilities category. Icons now prioritize facility use over building tags.
  • Changed the Korokoro Hiroba 3D model to a trampoline with brown netting. Menu buttons share the same CSS, and mobile drag handles also show the Komyaku pattern. Map-switch notifications now stay on one line.
